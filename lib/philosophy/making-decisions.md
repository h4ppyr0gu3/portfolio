# Making Decisions

Making decisions have to be there and then, setting deadlines or promises are not good

just start now, no point in delaying, it will always get more difficult over time

start now, it is obviously easier said than done, but most times it is a switch

Procrastination leads to a pile of laundry, and also an endless list of things that will be done 'one day', the best way is to just get started slowly, dip your foot in, prepare yourself and soon you will find your head is wet
