# Satisfying Answers

I have asked many people many questions, and a lot of them are superficial and meaningless questions whose answers are irrelevant, but there are some questions which you ponder and it is difficult to find a satisfying answer, by that I mean an answer that scratches the itch which the question posed.

I have a few such questions which i am still trying to find satisfying answers for and some for which i have found enough to satisfy me for now

## Questions

* What does it mean to know someone?
* What is happiness?
* What is the purpose of travelling?

More to come in future...

## Answers

#### What does it mean to know someone?&#x20;

Very often we say that we have friends or we know someone but to really know someone is really a thin line between the linguistic sense that you know them or that you know of them, so what does it really mean to know someone? The only answer I have been satisfied with so far is:

&#x20;Someone who in any given circumstance you know what they are likely to do
