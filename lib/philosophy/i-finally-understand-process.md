# I finally understand Process

For the longest time I absolutely rejected process and checking boxes

there is always some process to follow, but i am better, the way i will do it will work just fine, i don't need to worry about all the checks, some are meaningless

Well the reason is that we all have a tendency for our minds to wander, it is not meant to be an insult to anyone's intelligence but rather just a checklist to make sure we are all on the same page, and process also treats everyone the same, everyone has to follow the same steps
