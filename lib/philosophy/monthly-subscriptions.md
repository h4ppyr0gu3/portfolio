# Monthly Subscriptions

There is a common disdain for subscription services because of just how successful the business model has been, however, to subscribe to society is to subscribe to monthly subscriptions because whether you are running a company or a salaried employee you will have to manage your taxes or your building costs and maintenance

The only way to escape these subscriptions is to pay someone enough (monthly) to take care of it for you, may as well get used to it and enjoy paying the subscription to society

No matter where you are in life, it will always be a monthly subscription, there is no point in trying to hide it, instead, it should be treated as if you are taking a dump, spend a while on the toilet and enjoy the moment because hopefully you will have to take a dump everyday
