# The Reality of Anger

It is easy to get angry, at some point it is almost enjoyable, I thought that by getting angry I have the power to enact change, take for example road rage, you feel that these people better not mess with me because I am angry, how foolish are we to think that we have any impact on anything

No one cares, you are just a raging lunatic in the corner irrelevant to everything, you have no control over anything, we enjoy it because we think we have control we don't

Stay in your lane, shut up and stay calm

We aren't in control
